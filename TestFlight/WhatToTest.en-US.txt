The iPhone AI assistant now connects directly to your selected OpenAI or MiMo service. You can send a message without waiting for the Mac mini.

Open the gauge at the bottom of the composer. Tap the OpenAI model name to choose a different model, then adjust reasoning with the six-stop slider. Stops that the selected model does not support are dimmed. Switching to MiMo should show its original Low, Medium, and High settings.

Try a short arithmetic question, then attach a test image containing a number and ask the assistant to read it. If you report a problem, include the provider, model, slider setting, and error message. Please leave out API keys and private farm information.

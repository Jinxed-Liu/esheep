Fixes sheep poster sharing and individual sheep XLSX archive export being unavailable to administrators and workers. Both actions are available to farm members; whole-farm exports retain their existing role permissions.

This update improves sheep share posters, global farm names, and multi-photo browsing.

1. Upgrade and open your existing farm. Confirm your account, sheep, pens, photos, and history are preserved, then allow cloud sync to finish.
2. In Settings → Farm Name, save an optional English or Pinyin name. Reopen the page and check member sync. A blank value hides the corresponding poster text. Concurrent edits should prompt you to review the latest cloud values.
3. Switch between Magazine Cover, Breeding Card, and Minimal Photography. Check bilingual farm-name alignment, long names, the current brand icon, and photo layout.
4. Customize text, Chinese fonts, colors, position, layers, and photo crop. Save a personal template and reuse it for another sheep. Confirm current sheep and farm data are used and exported images match the preview.
5. Ewes should show reproductive metrics. Rams and sheep with unknown sex should show age, birth date, and current pen instead. Missing data must remain unspecified.
6. Browse multiple photos from sheep details. Check the initially selected photo, paging, dates, and avatar selection. Other photos should remain accessible when one fails to load.

For the existing internal testing group only. Apple distribution and real-device upgrade, sign-in, sync, and business acceptance are tracked separately.

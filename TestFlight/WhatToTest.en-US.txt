Focus on the settings avatar interactions.

1. Scroll up: the avatar should smoothly shrink, morph and merge with the Dynamic Island, then recover when scrolling back. Devices without an island, landscape and iPad should use the standard collapse effect.
2. First downward pull: the round avatar expands into a square with a spring and one haptic. Continuing the same drag should not open the viewer.
3. Release, then pull again: dragging from either the square photo or the settings list should open the full-screen viewer.
4. Test pinch zoom, double-tap zoom/reset, canceling a dismissal drag and dragging down to close. A zoomed image should pan normally.
5. Closing should restore the round avatar. Change avatar should use the existing crop and save flow.
6. Test Reduce Motion, disabled avatar motion and power saving. Expansion, viewing and closing should remain functional without visual jumps.

Check responsiveness, spring strength, haptic timing, rapid direction reversals and repeated opening/closing for photo flashes.

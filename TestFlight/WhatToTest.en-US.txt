This build focuses on farm renaming, name synchronization, and data preservation after upgrading.

1. The production farm is now named “吉昊羊场”. After upgrading and connecting, verify the latest name on Home, the farm list, Settings, and shared invitations. The name should update even when no new business records exist.
2. Only the farm owner can rename the farm under Settings → Farm name. Show cloud confirmation only after the server accepts the change. Offline or failed requests must not appear successful.
3. Rename from different devices and verify that stale edits require checking the latest cloud name. Old snapshots and delayed responses must not revert a newer name. Retrying the same request must not duplicate the rename.
4. Compare the original account, farm, sheep, pens, weighing, transfers, reproduction, and feeding history before and after upgrading. The farm ID and all business records must remain intact, and normal recording and cloud synchronization must continue working.
5. Reopen the app and check that the latest name remains. Widgets, search, and shortcuts should refresh with farm information. The external review copy must retain its existing name.

Automated validation, Apple processing, internal distribution, and physical-device upgrade acceptance are tracked separately. Internal testing only; external groups and tester membership remain unchanged.

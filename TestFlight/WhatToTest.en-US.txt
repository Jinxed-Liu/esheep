This build addresses a secure connection (SSL) error during sign-in on some cellular networks.

On an iPhone, turn off Wi-Fi and sign in with your existing account over LTE or 5G. Check that you reach the farm list or enter your farm. If this is the first download on this device, wait for the home screen to show your sheep records. You can also check sign-in on Wi-Fi.

If sign-in fails, report your carrier, iOS version, local time, the step that failed, and the error message. Hide email addresses and personal details in screenshots. Never include a password or verification code.

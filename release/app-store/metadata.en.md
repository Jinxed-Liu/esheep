## Description

eSheep+ is an iPhone and iPad production record system built for real sheep
farm workflows. Manage sheep and pens, record weights, health, breeding and
production events, maintain feed ingredients and formulas, run TMR batches,
and review analyses based on recorded facts.

The app works offline. When a cloud farm is enabled, owners can invite
administrators and workers with role-based access. Sync recovery relies on an
operation log, checkpoints and verified digests. Existing local or CloudKit
farms migrate to Supabase one farm at a time only after explicit confirmation.

Version 3.1 is completely free and contains no in-app purchases or
auto-renewable subscriptions.

Farm analyses support production decisions and do not replace veterinary
diagnosis, legal requirements or on-site judgment.

## Review Notes

Store the review account, verified-email steps and password only in App Store
Connect's secure Review Notes fields. Review path: register or Sign in with
Apple → create a farm → add a sheep → record weight/health → TMR → Settings for
cloud migration, invitations, export and account deletion.

Camera access captures sheep, symptoms, ear tags, supporting records and QR
codes. Location is used only to save a farm location and retrieve weather.
Photo access attaches media selected by the user. An account owner must export
and transfer or delete owned farms before requesting account deletion.

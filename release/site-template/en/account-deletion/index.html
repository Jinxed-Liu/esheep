Delete your eSheep+ account

eSheep+ 3.1 · Legal version 2026.09.01

Delete in the app

  1. Export anything you need from Data Import & Export in farm settings.
  2. If you still own a farm, transfer ownership or delete the owned farm first. This prevents damage to shared ledgers that other members still need.
  3. Open Settings → Account & Profile → Delete Account, review the effects, reauthenticate when requested, and confirm.
  4. Keep the deletion job ID. Once ownership blockers are resolved, active-database cleanup is targeted within 24 hours.

If you cannot sign in

Email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} from the registered address with “Delete eSheep+ account” in the subject. We request only the minimum verification data. Never send a password, verification code, API key, or complete identity-document image.

What is removed

The service revokes sessions and devices and removes profiles, memberships, personal sync content, and identifiable orphan attachments. Immutable operations that must remain for legal or shared-ledger integrity are de-identified. Backups and security logs roll off on the shortest configured cycle, target maximum 30 days unless law or an active incident requires longer.

Server deletion cannot erase local device data automatically. Remove local farms/conversations or uninstall the app after completion. Apple governs iCloud and device backups.

Cancellation and results

Deletion usually cannot be reversed once irreversible server cleanup begins. If ownership blocks the request, resolve ownership and submit again. For a result explanation or scope dispute, contact {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

Privacy Policy

eSheep+ 3.1.0 · Updated August 18, 2026

eSheep+ processes account names, email addresses, user and device identifiers, farm locations explicitly saved by users, farm production content and photos to provide authentication, sync, collaboration, recovery and support. We do not show ads, track across apps or sell personal data.

Storage and sharing

Cloud data is processed by Supabase Auth, Postgres, Storage and Realtime in Singapore. Realtime is only a delivery hint; database records, checkpoints and digests are authoritative. Farm data is available only to active members within their roles.

Your choices

You can export farm data, leave a shared farm or request account deletion in the app. Farm owners must first export and transfer or delete owned farms. After ownership blockers are resolved, deletion is targeted for completion within 24 hours.
